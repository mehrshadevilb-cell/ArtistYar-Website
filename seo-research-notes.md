The live ArtistYar homepage currently clearly states education in arrangement, mixing, mastering, theory, online classes, student outputs, FAQ, and RahYar AI. The current homepage title is «آموزش تنظیم، میکس و مسترینگ + راه‌یار AI | مهرشاد بنائی» and links to /courses, /online, /assistant, /free-player, /gallery.

SEO implication: the site has strong brand/entity signals but lacks a dedicated, deep, search-intent landing page targeting the exact query and supporting informational subtopics. A useful next implementation is a Persian evergreen guide/landing page with an exact-intent H1, practical curriculum, project-based proof, FAQs, internal links to courses and free lessons, and Article/Course/FAQ JSON-LD. Avoid keyword stuffing or guaranteeing first position.
